Everything You Need for Smarter Grocery Shopping

Powered by a four-component system designed for Malaysian households.

Content-Based Filtering

Cosine similarity matches your stated preferences (categories, halal status, budget) to grocery item feature vectors for personalised recommendations.

Collaborative Filtering

SVD++ item-based collaborative filtering learns from similar users' purchasing patterns to surface items you haven't discovered yet.

JAKIM Halal Verification

Every product is cross-checked against the JAKIM (Jabatan Kemajuan Islam Malaysia) halal certification database and displays an official reference number.

Price Comparison Engine

Compares prices in real time across AEON, Lotus's, and Mydin so you always know where to get the best deal on your groceries.

Budget-Aware Ranking

Set your monthly grocery budget and the system filters and ranks all recommendations to ensure you never overspend.

Hybrid Scoring Formula

Final score = 0.9 × max(Content Score, Collab Score) + 0.1 × Popularity, ensuring both personalisation and popularity are balanced.
